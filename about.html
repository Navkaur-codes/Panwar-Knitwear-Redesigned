01 — About

A Ludhiana
knitwear
manufacturer

Panwar Knitwear is a manufacturer based in Ludhiana, Punjab, specialising in knitted garments. We produce T-shirts, track pants, lowers, shorts, sweatshirts and hoodies under our two brands, ZONIXA and MSP Sports.

With over 15 years in garment manufacturing, we supply 1000+ distributors, working directly with retailers, wholesalers and clothing brands — no middlemen between our factory and your shop.

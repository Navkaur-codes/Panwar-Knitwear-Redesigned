## Business Identity
- Business: Panwar Knitwear
- Location: Ludhiana, Punjab, India
- Business type: Knitwear / garment manufacturer and wholesale supplier
- Primary focus: B2B, wholesale, bulk and business enquiries
- Local market: Ludhiana and Punjab
- Secondary market: buyers across India and relevant wholesale/export enquiries

The website should feel like a **real Ludhiana-based apparel manufacturer**, not a generic fashion e-commerce template.

## Design Principles
The redesign must always be:
- Modern
- Clean
- Visual
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Product-focused
- Mobile-friendly
- Easy to scan
- Appropriate for a manufacturing/wholesale business

The visual identity should combine:
**Ludhiana textile manufacturing + modern apparel**

Avoid:
- Generic templates
- Excessive gradients
- Neon styling
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Unnecessary luxury styling
- Excessive animations
- Large blocks of text
- Visually repetitive sections

Prefer:
**Strong imagery + concise copy + clear CTAs + visual hierarchy**

## Local-First Positioning
Make Ludhiana/Punjab presence clearly visible.

The site should naturally communicate:
- Ludhiana-based manufacturing
- Wholesale/bulk availability
- Easy local contact
- Product discovery
- Direct business enquiry

Local SEO should be natural and never keyword-stuffed.

Relevant terms may include:
- Knitwear manufacturer in Ludhiana
- T-shirt manufacturer in Ludhiana
- Wholesale garments in Ludhiana
- Sportswear manufacturer
- Hoodie manufacturer
- Track pants manufacturer

## Content Accuracy
Never invent or assume:
- Years of experience
- Production capacity
- Customer/client counts
- Certifications
- Awards
- Reviews/testimonials
- Export markets
- Revenue
- Client logos
- Product specifications
- Prices

Use existing verified information or clearly leave the information out.

## Images
Prioritize genuine:
1. Panwar Knitwear product images
2. Factory/manufacturing images
3. ZONIXA/MSP Sports images

Use temporary stock/placeholder imagery only when necessary and make it easy to replace.

Do not make the website dependent on random stock imagery.

## Technical Rules
- Preserve the existing project's appropriate technology/structure unless there is a strong reason to change it.
- Keep code simple and understandable.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies and over-engineering.
- Use semantic HTML and clean CSS.
- Use JavaScript only when genuinely useful.
- Keep the site responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop.
- No horizontal scrolling.
- Optimize images and avoid unnecessarily heavy effects.

## UX / Conversion Rules
Important actions should always be easy to find:
- WhatsApp
- Call
- Get Quote
- Product enquiry

On mobile, a persistent **Call / WhatsApp / Quote** action can be used where appropriate.

Forms should remain short and practical.

## SEO & Accessibility
Maintain:
- Proper page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Semantic heading hierarchy
- Descriptive alt text
- Accessible buttons/links
- Good contrast
- Mobile responsiveness
- Local business information

## Redesign Rule
Do not redesign blindly.

Before changing major sections:
1. Understand the existing website and its useful information.
2. Preserve factual business content.
3. Improve hierarchy, usability and visual presentation.
4. Remove repetition and unnecessary text.
5. Do not change the business positioning without a clear reason.
